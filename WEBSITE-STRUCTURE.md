## ABOUT

### Section Title
`About`

### Opening Statement

> The spaces we inhabit shape who we are — whether that space is a city, a room, or our own inner landscape.

### Bio

Marta Rodríguez Fernández is a Spanish architect, design researcher, professor, and writer based in Berkeley, California. Her work explores how architecture and everyday spaces influence health, learning, relationships, and quality of life.

Born in the mountains of Asturias, Marta attended a one-room schoolhouse in rural northern Spain — an experience that shaped her belief that architecture begins not with buildings, but with the spaces where life happens.

After studying architecture at the University of Valladolid and the Polytechnic University of Madrid (ETSAM), she worked at OMA/Rem Koolhaas in Rotterdam, contributing to the Rothschild Bank headquarters in London and the Córdoba Congress Center. She directed projects at Lahoz Lopez Arquitectos in Madrid before moving to Japan, where she conducted research on small spaces and transformability, interviewing architects Toyo Ito and Kazuyo Sejima.

In 2014, Marta joined the University of Houston as an architecture professor, earning tenure in 2020. She has been a Visiting Scholar at UC Berkeley's Center for Japanese Studies, a Visiting Fellow at Sciences Po in Paris, and a Visiting Professor at TU Graz in Austria.

Her research has produced two original concepts that have shaped contemporary discourse: **Petite Architecture** — the idea that furniture and small spaces can carry the same spatial ambition as buildings — and **Urban Domesticity** — a design ethic in which care, aesthetics, and emotional intimacy are treated as urban values.

She is the author of *Charlotte Perriand & Kazuyo Sejima: Una Revolución Sutil* (2021) and is completing *The Habitable City: Inclusive Human-Scale Urban Design* (2027). Her work has been published in the *Athens Journal of Architecture*, WIT Press, and Cambridge Scholars Publishing, and she has been invited to contribute a chapter to Harvard Graduate School of Design.

In 2023, after overcoming cancer, Marta underwent a personal transformation that deepened her design philosophy. "At 43, I was reborn," she writes. "I let the ego die so I could begin to live."

Today, she works across six interconnected projects — design advisory, architecture, urban research, a newsletter, a spirituality platform, and a community garden — all unified by the question: *How can we design a better life?*

### Philosophy (Three Pillars)

**Elegance**
Refined simplicity. Spaces that breathe — where every choice is intentional and nothing is superfluous.

**Economy**
Purposeful use of what is already there. Every element earns its place through function and beauty.

**Ecology**
Harmony between space and self. Choices that are conscious of material, longevity, and life.

### Behavior

- **Desktop:** Thumbnail on left, title and phrase on right
- **Mobile:** Thumbnail on top, title and phrase below
- **Click:** Opens external site (drmarta.design, habitable.studio, etc.)
- **Hover:** Subtle opacity change or underline on title

### Image Requirements

Each project needs one thumbnail:
- **Aspect ratio:** 4:3 or 16:9
- **Size:** 800x600px (or 1200x675px for 16:9)
- **Style:** Clean, architectural, consistent treatment

**Recommended Images:**
| Project | Image Idea |

### Visual Rules

1. **No borders or shadows** on thumbnails
2. **Consistent image treatment** — same aspect ratio, same filter (if any)
3. **Generous spacing** between items (40-60px)
4. **Title:** Bold, linked, 18-20px
5. **Phrase:** Regular weight, muted color, 14-16px
6. **No extra text** — just thumbnail, title, phrase

## Technical Notes

### Navigation Behavior

- Fixed top navigation
- Transparent on hero section
- Solid background on scroll (white or dark)
- Smooth scroll to sections
- Active state highlights current section

### Section Transitions

- Generous padding between sections (100-150px)
- Subtle dividers or background color changes
- Consistent visual rhythm

### Responsive Design

**Desktop (1024px+):**
- Full-width sections
- 2-column grids for projects, writing, speaking
- Generous white space

**Tablet (768-1024px):**
- Adjusted grids
- Slightly reduced padding
- Maintained hierarchy

**Mobile (0-768px):**
- Single column
- Stacked layouts
- Touch-friendly targets (44px minimum)
- Hamburger menu for navigation

### Performance Targets

- LCP: < 2.5s
- FID: < 100ms
- CLS: < 0.1

## Implementation Options

### Option A: Webflow
- Custom design
- Smooth animations
- CMS for Writing section
- Easy to maintain

### Option B: Squarespace
- Template-based
- Faster setup
- Limited customization
- Built-in CMS

### Option C: Custom (HTML/CSS/JS)
- Full control
- Fastest performance
- Requires developer
- Most flexible

### Recommendation
**Webflow** — Best balance of design control, CMS capabilities, and maintainability for a single-page site with this much content.
